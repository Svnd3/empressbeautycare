Questions

Good to know.

Still unsure? Call 0720 016 178 or send a booking request.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, subject to stylist and service availability. A quick WhatsApp message helps reduce your wait.

How do I book?

Choose your branch and service in the booking form. It creates a ready-to-send WhatsApp message; your slot is confirmed once the Empress team replies.

What time do you close?

The studios are open daily, but closing times can vary. Confirm the day’s hours on WhatsApp before setting off.

Can I request more than one service?

Absolutely. Select the closest category, then list every service you want in the notes so the team can plan your visit.

Are the prices on this page final?

They are starting points from recent social offers. Hair length, product, size and style can affect the final quote, so confirm before travelling.
