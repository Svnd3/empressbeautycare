# Asset provenance and launch permissions

## Fonts

- Manrope is self-hosted from the Google Fonts distribution. Its OFL licence is stored at `public/fonts/MANROPE-LICENSE.txt`.
- DM Serif Display is self-hosted from the Google Fonts distribution. Its OFL licence is stored at `public/fonts/DM-SERIF-DISPLAY-LICENSE.txt`.
- Apple SF fonts from the supplied reference were inspected only and are not included.

## Empress imagery

The Empress wordmark and woman silhouette were supplied directly by the user for this concept. `empress-logo.png` is the transparent full lockup; `empress-icon.png` is a transparent crop of the same supplied woman mark for the loader and favicon. No replacement mark was generated.

The compressed WebP files in `public/images/` are demonstration crops made from current public Empress Beautycare profile/media thumbnails. They are included in this private sales concept to show authentic art direction, not as a claim of licence for a public commercial launch.

Public posting does not make an image public-domain. Before public deployment, obtain written Empress approval and confirm photographer plus identifiable client/model consent. Request original high-resolution files; do not rely on social thumbnails. TikTok/Instagram music or video rights do not transfer to an off-platform website.

Raw downloaded thumbnails are deliberately excluded from Git. The final processed images remain in the private concept so the owner can evaluate the real visual direction.

## Customer comments

The three short quotations in the “From the Empress chair” section are exact public comments on official Empress TikTok posts. Each card includes a direct source link and is labelled as a TikTok comment; no star score or aggregate rating is inferred. Obtain written permission from the commenters before a final commercial launch or replace them with owner-approved customer reviews.

## Maps

The location finder embeds OpenStreetMap and retains its visible contributor attribution. Simara Mall and Morovian Mall use corroborated OpenStreetMap coordinates; RNG Plaza intentionally shows the surrounding Ronald Ngala Street area because a definitive owner-claimed pin was not established. The directions actions use Google Maps search URLs rather than claiming unverified coordinates.

## Reference sites

The supplied Apple and barbershop folders are excluded from Git. Their layout principles were studied, but none of their images, videos, copy, proprietary fonts, testimonials, logos, or source code are shipped in this project.
