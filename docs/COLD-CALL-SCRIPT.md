# Cold-call package for Empress Beautycare

Use this only as an honest unsolicited pitch. Do not imply Empress commissioned the concept, promise bookings or rankings, or quote combined followers as unique people.

## 20–30 second opener

> Hi, is this the person who handles Empress Beautycare’s marketing? I’m [NAME]. You’ve built a strong audience—your main TikTok has more than 200,000 followers and Instagram has more than 50,000—but I couldn’t find one credible official website that brings RNG Plaza, Simara Mall and the Ruiru branch together. I’ve designed a working website concept specifically for Empress. Could I take 30 seconds to explain why?

If they say yes, move into one or two discovery questions before pitching.

## Discovery questions

Choose two or three and listen fully.

1. When a new client finds a hairstyle on TikTok, how do they know which branch and WhatsApp number to use?
2. Do most bookings currently start through DMs, WhatsApp or phone calls?
3. Which questions does your team answer repeatedly: location, services, availability or prices?
4. Which branch or service would you most like to fill with more appointments?
5. Do you currently have one place you control where clients can see all three branches and booking options?
6. Who besides you would need to approve the website and the photos used?

## 60-second pitch

> From what I found, Empress already has the hard part: attention and proof. The gap is what happens after somebody sees a post. Your branch details and booking paths are spread across profiles, bios and captions, and I couldn’t find a credible official site customers can use as the single source of truth.
>
> The concept I built gives Empress one premium, mobile-first home: clear services, approved work, and separate directions and contact actions for RNG Plaza, Simara Mall and Morovian Mall in Ruiru. A visitor can move from a social post to the right branch, then call, open Maps or WhatsApp the correct number without hunting through captions. It complements TikTok and Instagram; it does not replace them.
>
> I’d like to show you the actual site on your phone—it takes about seven minutes. Would [SPECIFIC DAY/TIME] or [ALTERNATIVE] work better?

## The live-demo path

Keep the demo under seven minutes:

1. Start on the mobile hero: “This makes the full offer clear in seconds.”
2. Swipe the service cards: “Social inspires; this helps people choose.”
3. Show the qualified price guide: “You decide whether prices stay, change, or disappear.”
4. Open the three branch cards: “No more guessing which number or location.”
5. Choose Ruiru and submit a test booking: “The form routes the complete request to 0796 350 593 and clearly says it is not confirmed until your team replies.”
6. Close with ownership: “Once approved, this becomes an Empress asset you control.”

## Objections

### “Instagram and TikTok already work.”

> Absolutely—and that audience is the reason this is worthwhile. Social creates discovery; the site gives that traffic one official destination with all three locations and the correct booking route. It strengthens what already works.

### “Just send it on WhatsApp.”

> Happy to. I’ll send the preview now. It makes more sense with a two-minute walkthrough, so may I call at [TIME] after you’ve opened it?

### “We’re not interested.”

> Understood. Before I leave it, is that because a website is not a priority, or because you already have somebody handling it? I’ll only send the preview if it is useful.

### “How much?”

> The cost depends on whether you want the finished core site only or extras such as hosting and ongoing updates. I’ll show you the result first, agree the exact scope, then put every setup and recurring cost in writing—nothing assumed.

Do not improvise a price during the call unless your actual commercial terms are already decided.

### “We already have a developer or website.”

> That’s good. I couldn’t identify a credible official site linked across the public profiles, so the immediate opportunity may simply be making it visible and consistent. You can still use this concept as a second opinion.

### “We handle bookings on WhatsApp.”

> Perfect—the site does not replace WhatsApp. It filters the basic questions and routes each visitor into the correct WhatsApp conversation with one complete request.

### “We don’t want prices online.”

> That’s completely fine. Services can stay visible without fixed prices, current offers can remain on social, and every enquiry can go to WhatsApp for a quote.

### “Where did you get our photos?”

> The private preview uses public Empress references for demonstration. I would not publish those commercially without your approval. The live version should use original files you authorise, with photographer and client/model consent.

### “I need to ask my partner or manager.”

> Of course. Who should join a short demo? I can send one link and arrange a time when both of you are available.

### “How do I know this is legitimate?”

> Fair question. This is an unsolicited concept, not an official Empress project yet. I’ll send my details and a view-only preview; you do not need to share passwords or pay anything just to review it.

## Close and next step

Ask for one concrete commitment: a demo time, the correct decision-maker, or permission to send the link. If they decline twice, thank them and stop. If they are interested, send a written scope that separates the build fee, domain/hosting, maintenance, content responsibilities, and launch approvals.
