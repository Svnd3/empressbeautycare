# Empress Beautycare research and fit assessment

Research checked on **3 September 2026**. Public information changes; figures below are a dated snapshot, not permanent claims.

## Executive verdict

**Fit score: 9/10 — a high-value, suitable prospect.**

Empress has already done the difficult part: it has attention, a broad service catalogue, visible ongoing work, and three operating studios. The missing layer is an owned, credible place that turns that attention into the correct branch, directions, contact number, and booking request.

The site is worth pitching because it solves specific, observable problems:

- The main TikTok profile has more than 200,000 followers and the Instagram profile has more than 50,000, yet no credible standalone official website was found.
- Three branches and two phone numbers are spread across bios, captions, profiles, and older directory records.
- Legacy addresses and conflicting public hours can create avoidable uncertainty.
- A wide and frequently changing service menu is difficult to navigate in a social feed.
- There is no strong, reliable independent review hub, so the website cites a small set of real public TikTok comments instead of inventing ratings or review totals.
- A branch-aware WhatsApp request reduces repeated questions and routes Ruiru enquiries to its distinct number.

The website will only reach its potential if the team keeps branch data and offers current, replies reliably on WhatsApp, and supplies approved original media. The design cannot compensate for slow replies or uneven in-studio delivery.

## Hours, walk-ins, and discrepancies

- A current official post says Monday–Sunday, 8am–9pm.
- Current posts state that walk-ins and bookings are accepted.
- Happy-hour captions conflict: one says 8am–12pm Monday–Saturday and all Sunday; another says it begins at 7am.
- An older Instagram post gives a different Sunday start time.

For that reason, the concept says **“Open daily · Walk-ins welcome · confirm today’s closing time on WhatsApp”** and does not publish fixed hours or happy-hour terms. Obtain a written schedule from the client before launch.

## Design and conversion rationale

The supplied references were used as design research, not copied assets:

- From Apple: calm spacing, confident type scale, neutral bands, restrained motion, 44px targets, scroll-snap browsing, and accessible details.
- From the barbershop reference: image-led service discovery, a deep beauty-service catalogue, prominent location choice, and direct WhatsApp conversion.
- From Empress itself: champagne/gold signage, cream interiors, a dark wordmark, real service work, and “all services under one roof.”

The result avoids the reference barbershop’s gender toggle, electric pink/blue palette, giant morphing blob, fake form flow, inherited testimonials, legacy dependencies, and Million Hairs assets. It also avoids Apple’s proprietary assets, code, and SF fonts.

## Recommended next actions

1. Show the working concept on a phone, centred on the branch-to-WhatsApp flow.
2. Confirm the business owner, decision-makers, branch details, opening hours, and price policy.
3. Confirm approval for the supplied logo and request 12–20 approved, original-resolution photos covering each branch and service.
4. Agree who owns offer updates and how quickly WhatsApp enquiries should be answered.
5. Claim or correct all three Google Business profiles and start a permission-based review process.
6. Connect a domain, privacy-respecting analytics, and Search Console only after approval.

## Research limitations

Public social metadata, search indexes, embeds, and directory mirrors were used for corroboration. Social counts and offers are volatile. Directory mirrors are not treated as authoritative or as media-license sources. No private records, internal booking data, or client confirmation were available during this assessment.
